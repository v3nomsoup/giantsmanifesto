## What each milestone delivers

1. **Foundation & public content site.** The Astro app in `site/` renders the manifesto from the repo-root Markdown file, with homepage (including "Who's behind this"), changelog, contact, privacy and imprint pages, translation-ready under `/en/`. It ships as a Docker image to Coolify. CI runs lint, type check and tests, and deploys only when green. Feedback points to GitHub issues until milestone 4.
2. **Database, email & email signing.** SQLite with migrations on startup, the mail queue over SMTP, and signing and withdrawing via email link. Public signature list and live counts on the homepage. Honeypot, rate limits and ALTCHA on forms.
3. **Provider sign-in.** GitHub, LinkedIn, Mastodon-API servers and Bluesky for signing and withdrawing, with the short-lived identity cookie and confirmation form.
4. **Organizations, feedback & admin.** Organization applications with domain-matched email confirmation, logo upload, manual approval; the feedback form; the admin area behind the GitHub allowlist.
5. **Backups, retention & launch.** Nightly local backups, daily `age`-encrypted email backups, retention clean-up jobs, operator docs and the manual launch checklist.
